About Us

Where Effort Turns Into Results

F5 Fitness Studio Unisex A/C is an air-conditioned gym and physical fitness center on Muthirapalayam Road, Pondicherry. Train with free weights, strength machines, battle ropes and cardio, with programs for weight loss, weight gain and strength.
